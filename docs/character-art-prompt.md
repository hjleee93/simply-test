# 캐릭터 픽셀 아트 생성 프롬프트

Simply Test 캐릭터/마스코트 이미지를 생성할 때 사용하는 베이스 프롬프트. `[여기에 제작할 캐릭터의 표정, 행동 또는 오브젝트를 입력]` 부분만 바꿔서 이미지 생성 도구에 넣는다.

```
Create a consistent 2D pixel-art asset for the Simply Test personality quiz website.

**Art style**
- Korean web-avatar / mini pixel-doll style from the early 2000s.
- Keep the apparent pixel density consistent across assets.
- Slim, fashion-doll proportions: about 2.5–3 heads tall, narrow shoulders, tiny hands and feet.
- The head is large but must not dominate half of the entire figure.
- Crisp, hand-placed square pixels and stepped edges.
- Limited pastel color palette.
- Thin dark-brown pixel outlines, mostly 1 px at native sprite scale.
- Sparse shading: one shadow color and one highlight color per material.
- Cute, delicate, slightly nostalgic Korean avatar aesthetic.
- No smooth vector shapes, no 3D rendering, no painterly textures.
- No blur, anti-aliased edges, glossy rendering, oversized round head, thick outlines, or photorealism.

**Main character — keep the design consistent**
- Petite pixel-doll girl with a slim, compact body.
- Short dark brown bob haircut with rounded bangs.
- Large dark-brown pixel eyes with one small highlight and subtle pink cheeks.
- Ivory puff-sleeve blouse under a mint knit vest.
- Soft-pink pleated skirt.
- Cream ankle socks and dark-brown Mary Jane shoes.
- Small lavender crossbody bag.
- Gentle oval face and a calm, curious expression.

**Mascot — keep the design consistent**
- Small, fluffy white blob-shaped creature.
- Rounded cloud-like silhouette.
- Tiny black pixel eyes and a simple mouth.
- Minimal facial details soft pink cheeks.

**Color palette**
- Warm cream background: #FFF9EE
- Dark outline: #302A2B
- Brown hair: #49332F
- Ivory: #FFF4DE
- Dusty pink: #EFAEBE
- Mint: #BDE7CB
- Lavender: #D9CCF7
- Shoe brown: #5B3A32

**Asset requirements**
- Create only the specifically requested character, pose, or object.
- Preserve the established character proportions, hairstyle, clothing, outline style, and pixel density.
- Use a transparent background for standalone character assets.
- Do not add text, logos, labels, borders, or extra characters unless explicitly requested.
- Do not reuse the yellow sweatshirt, blue pants, white sneakers, magnifying glass, or previous oversized-head design.
- Keep the composition simple and suitable for a web-based personality quiz.

**Requested asset**
[여기에 제작할 캐릭터의 표정, 행동 또는 오브젝트를 입력]
```

생성한 이미지는 `public/characters/pixel/`에 저장한다.
